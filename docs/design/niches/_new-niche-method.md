# Method for any new niche
1. Risk: money, identity, health, children or legal means fintech trust and copy rules.
2. Buying moment: impulse (food, games, consumer) means media-first hero, short copy, motion 3; considered (B2B, health, education) means product or editorial hero, more proof, motion 2.
3. Study 3 best-in-class live sites plus 8+ references; note shared palette temperature, type style, imagery and density.
4. Choose a base pack: playful (consumer, food, events), professional (B2B, education, health providers), fintech (regulated), gaming (entertainment, Web3 collectibles).
5. Override only accent, neutral temperature, fonts, radius, imagery, motion level, required sections and voice, in niches/<name>/design-system.md.
6. Add niche content (menus and delivery times; course outcomes; clinician credentials and privacy; wallet support and audits).
7. Run the QA checklist unchanged.

Example, food delivery (QuickBite): playful base, motion 3. Ink brown #2A211D (confirmed); cream, paper, orange and green proposed as #FBF3E6, #FFFDF8, #E8590C, #2F7D4F until real tokens are pasted. Editorial serif or bold condensed caps for headlines with a friendly sans body. Real local dishes with warm grading and alt text naming the dish. Cards show time range, rating, fee and average order in tabular figures. Intro loader under 1.5s with skip.
