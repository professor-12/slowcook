# Fintech PRD additions
Goals: signup, waitlist, demo request, app download.
Required: regulator and licence details, a security page (encryption, data handling, certifications with named auditors), real fees or an honest "not announced", legal operator, support contact, privacy and terms, complaints process where regulated.
Trust sits at decision points (hero proof line, beside signup CTAs, in pricing), not only in the footer. Cover institutional, security and transactional trust.
Forms: step indicator, inline validation, saved progress, clear consent.
Motion level 2 on marketing, 1 in forms and dashboards; no smooth scroll. Client signs off every rate, claim and disclaimer.
