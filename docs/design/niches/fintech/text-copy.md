# Fintech copy overrides
Precise and calm; every claim checkable, numbers with units, dates and conditions. Hero: what it does, for whom, the control or saving ("Verify once. Approve every share."). State availability, pricing and partner status honestly. Name security standards instead of "bank-grade security" (e.g. encryption standard plus audit and auditor). "No hidden fees" only with a linked fee table. CTAs: "Open an account", "Talk to sales", "See pricing". Money errors say what did and did not happen ("Payment not sent. Your account was not charged."). Avoid "guaranteed returns", "risk-free", and "instant" unless true.
